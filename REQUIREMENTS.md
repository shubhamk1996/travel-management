# Functional requirements and contracts

## Data and persistence

- The dashboard lists trips and supports creation; a trip has a name, destination, inclusive start and end dates, USD budget, travellers, activities, bookings and expenses. A trip can be viewed, edited and deleted after confirmation. Dates must be ordered and span at most 366 inclusive days. The destination is descriptive text.
- Trip status is Upcoming before the first date, In progress on any trip date, and Completed after the last date, using the browser's local date.
- JSON Server exposes `/trips` and `/trips/:id`. Each trip and its nested records form one JSON document. The UI sends a whole-document PUT for each change and displays API errors. Data persists in `db.json`. This single-user server does not coordinate concurrent clients.
- Record IDs are strings. Dates are `yyyy-MM-dd`; times are local `HH:mm`, with no time-zone conversion. Amounts in JSON are integer USD cents. Input amounts must be non-negative, have at most two decimal places, and be no greater than $9,000,000,000.00. Durations are whole minutes from 1 to 1440 and events must end by midnight.
- Undo and redo retain up to 50 prior trip snapshots in the current page session. Booking edits and accepted rescheduling moves are one operation. A new change clears redo; both undo and redo save the resulting trip to the API. History is not retained across navigation or refresh.

## Itinerary and bookings

- The itinerary displays one day for every trip date. An activity has a name, predefined location, date, start, duration, estimated cost, opening and closing times, and fixed/flexible flag. The closing time must be later than opening. Add, edit and delete activities; activities made out-of-range by a later trip edit remain visible for correction.
- Drag a flexible activity onto another flexible activity to exchange dates and start times, or onto a day to retain its time on that day. Earlier/later buttons exchange slots with an adjacent activity. Fixed activities cannot be dragged or swapped. Any conflicts caused by a manual move remain visible for correction.
- A booking has a name, type (flight, train, hotel, cab), status (confirmed, pending, cancelled), predefined location, date, start time, duration and cost. Editing permits delay and cancellation. Pending and confirmed non-hotel bookings block time; cancelled bookings do not. Hotels count toward budget but do not block an entire stay or participate in time/travel conflicts. Booking dates outside the trip are flagged. An expense-linked booking cannot be deleted until its expenses are deleted or unlinked.
- The predefined location and symmetric travel-time matrix lives in `src/domain.ts`. Times are in minutes and are synthetic, not route estimates. Locations without a matrix entry are reported as unavailable rather than assumed to have zero travel time.
- Conflicts report overlapping activities and active non-hotel bookings, insufficient travel time between consecutive events on a day, dates outside the trip, and activities outside opening hours. Messages identify affected items and the required travel time. The app allows manual edits that introduce conflicts but reports them.
- Changing a booking's date, start time or duration, or cancelling an active booking, opens a preview. Starting with that booking change, the scheduler considers flexible activities involved in conflicts in itinerary order. It searches each trip day in order, minute by minute within the activity's opening hours, for the first slot that resolves that activity's conflicts and introduces no new conflict among other items. It does not move fixed activities or bookings. The preview lists moves and flexible activities for which no slot was found with other current positions held fixed, and reports remaining conflicts. Apply saves the booking and moves together; discard saves neither. The greedy search does not attempt every possible combination of rearrangements.

## Budget and group expenses

- Estimated plans equal the sum of all activity estimates plus pending and confirmed booking costs. Actual expenses equal recorded expenses plus confirmed booking costs that have no linked expense. When one or more expenses reference a booking, their total replaces that booking's cost in actual spending, avoiding duplicate counting. A linked expense remains an actual payment if its booking is later cancelled; edit or remove it to record a refund. Remaining budget is total budget minus actual spending. The UI warns separately when actual spending or estimated plans exceed budget and charts actual amounts by category.
- Expenses have a title, category, non-negative amount, payer, selected participants, split mode and optional linked booking. A payer and at least one participant are required. A traveller referenced by an expense cannot be removed until that expense is changed or removed; editing a traveller's name retains their ID and references.
- Equal splitting divides integer cents evenly, assigning leftover cents in participant selection order. Custom amounts must sum exactly to the expense. Percentage inputs use hundredths of one percent and must total exactly 100.00%. Percentage shares use largest-remainder rounding, with participant order breaking ties, so shares always total the original cents.
- A traveller's balance is their payments minus their shares. Positive balances are owed to them; negative balances are owed by them. Suggested transfers greedily match debtors and creditors in traveller order and sum exactly to the outstanding balances. Settlements are informational; there is no payment processing.

## Interface and verification

- The responsive interface provides dashboard, trip editing, overview, itinerary, bookings, budget and expenses, and travellers and settlements. Forms validate input, confirmations guard deletion, and loading, empty, error and success states are shown.
- Unit tests exercise date boundaries, money, splits, budgets, settlements, conflicts and scheduling. React integration tests exercise trip form validation and creation. Playwright tests use a separate deterministic fixture to exercise creation, refresh persistence, drag and drop, undo/redo, delay/cancellation previews, expense validation and itinerary CRUD.
